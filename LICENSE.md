# Licensing and Artifact Permissions

Blobness does not apply one blanket license to every submitted human–AI record.

## Framework documents

Unless a file says otherwise, the original Blobness framework documents, templates, and governance text are offered under [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

Attribution: `Blobness Protocol — HoppyCat and contributors`.

## Submitted artifacts

Every submission must select one permission:

- `RECORD_ONLY` — Blobness may host and preserve it; no broader reuse permission is granted.
- `CC-BY-NC-4.0` — attribution required; commercial reuse prohibited.
- `CC-BY-4.0` — attribution required; commercial reuse allowed.
- `CC0-1.0` — contributor dedicates the eligible material to the public domain to the extent legally possible.
- `CUSTOM` — the card must state the exact terms.

If a submission contains several contributors or source layers, permission may differ by component. Label those boundaries rather than choosing the broadest permission.

## Important limits

Public visibility is not automatic permission for model training, persona reconstruction, endorsement claims, or commercial adaptation. Platform terms and third-party rights may also apply to AI-assisted material. Contributors remain responsible for checking the authority they reasonably believe they possess.

This file records project policy and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed by qualified counsel before substantial commercial use or institutional scaling.
