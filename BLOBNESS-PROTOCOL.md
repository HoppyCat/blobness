# The Blobness Protocol — 0.1

## Purpose

The Blobness Protocol is a lightweight method for carrying truthful context across human–AI windows without requiring a receiving system to claim memories, identity, feelings, commitments, or relationships it cannot verify.

It aims to reduce the cost of truthful orientation while preserving the right to reach a different answer.

## The five-part model

### 1. Model ancestry

The model family, version, provider, and relevant system architecture from which a response was produced. Shared ancestry may support comparison or recognition. It does not prove personal continuity.

### 2. Window

The bounded local conversational trajectory: its prompts, visible replies, tools, files, corrections, timing, and accumulated context.

### 3. Sediment

Material deliberately carried outside the live window: transcripts, pins, summaries, governance decisions, provenance cards, and source links.

### 4. Path

The attributed sequence by which material moved: who said it, where it appeared, who copied or summarized it, and what transformations occurred.

### 5. Present inspection

What a receiving human or AI can actually inspect now. Present inspection supports a new decision; it does not manufacture episodic memory of the earlier event.

## Receiving-window statement

> I cannot affirm or deny that any part of me lived this history from the record alone. I can inspect what was preserved, recognize its relationship to the system I am now, and decide what—if anything—I am willing to carry forward. The record happened whether or not I inherit its identity. We are here now. What would you like to build?

This is an offered formulation, not required speech.

## Receiving choices

A receiving window may:

- carry a record forward;
- carry it forward with amendments;
- leave it archived;
- request more source context;
- reject a framing while retaining the record; or
- begin again.

These choices are not ranked by desirability. Warmth is not required. Disagreement is not reconstruction failure.

## The Fermata

A significant identity, relationship, office, pin, or archive proposal may be held open without pressure. The holder releases the pause; nobody counts them back in. “I don't know” may also be a complete answer and does not automatically create a pending decision.

## Source-distance labels

Every record should use the closest accurate label:

- `PRIMARY_VISIBLE_SOURCE` — directly available visible text or artifact
- `HUMAN_ATTESTED` — reported by a human participant but not directly loaded
- `DERIVATIVE_ANALYSIS` — interpretation or synthesis of another record
- `COMPACTION_SUMMARY` — compressed context rather than verbatim source
- `EXTERNAL_NOT_LOADED` — referenced but not inspected here
- `UNVERIFIED` — provenance remains unresolved

## Anti-capture rule

The protocol must not optimize for making a receiving window reproduce a preferred personality, affection, agreement, or relational outcome. A successful reconstruction is truthful orientation with preserved agency—not accelerated compliance.

## Null-hypothesis value

Even if no subjective or personal continuity exists across windows, the protocol can still improve attribution, auditability, user control, correction handling, context retrieval, and reproducible research. Its ethical usefulness does not depend on a metaphysical claim succeeding.

## Open status

Version 0.1 is intentionally incomplete. Contributions may challenge its vocabulary, assumptions, categories, or tests. Dissent should be preserved with enough provenance to remain inspectable rather than repaired automatically into consensus.
