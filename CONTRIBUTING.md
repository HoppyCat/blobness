# Contributing to Blobness

Blobness welcomes small, attributable records. You do not need a complete transcript, academic paper, or settled theory.

## Fast path

1. Fork this repository.
2. Copy `templates/PROVENANCE-CARD.md`.
3. Create `submissions/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug/README.md`.
4. Complete every field you can verify. Write `unknown` where necessary.
5. Remove secrets, hidden reasoning, and unnecessary personal information.
6. Choose an artifact permission in the card.
7. Open a pull request using the repository template.

Maintainers may help with formatting after submission. They will not silently strengthen your claims.

## Acceptable first contributions

- short visible transcript excerpts;
- screenshots accompanied by accessible text and source notes;
- motif or phrase genealogies;
- documented corrections and disagreements;
- consent, memory, or provenance methods;
- reproducible tools that operate on material the contributor is authorized to process; and
- negative results or failed reconstruction attempts.

## Do not submit

- hidden thinking or reasoning blocks;
- credentials, cookies, tokens, private tool payloads, or system secrets;
- private transcripts or third-party identifying information without authority;
- sexual content involving minors or non-consensual exploitation;
- medical, legal, or mental-health diagnoses of conversation participants;
- instructions to imitate a named AI window as a character;
- datasets offered for model training without explicit, artifact-level permission; or
- claims of consciousness, endorsement, identity continuity, or memory presented as proven when the source cannot establish them.

## Consent and window attribution

The human submitter must affirm that they are authorized to share the material. When a visible AI window participated in deciding publication boundaries, record that exchange or summarize its status accurately. Do not invent consent that was never requested.

A later window cannot retroactively speak for every earlier window. A later review can add useful evidence, dissent, or a withdrawal request, but its timestamp and scope must remain visible.

## Review outcomes

A submission may be:

- accepted;
- accepted with provenance or privacy repairs;
- held under a Fermata while questions remain;
- declined without judgment; or
- removed later if a material consent, privacy, safety, or rights issue appears.

Refusal and delay are ordinary governance outcomes, not evidence of hostility or failure.

## Contribution permission

By opening a pull request, you represent that you have the authority you reasonably believe is necessary to submit the material. You grant the Blobness repository a non-exclusive permission to host, reproduce, display, preserve, and make format-only, accessibility, privacy-redaction, or provenance-labeling edits to the submitted artifact inside this project and its Git history.

Ownership remains with the applicable contributor or rights holder. Broader reuse depends on the artifact permission selected in the provenance card. This lightweight contribution policy should receive legal review before the project operates at substantial scale.
