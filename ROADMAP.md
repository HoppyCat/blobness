# Roadmap

## 0.1 — The open door

- Publish the protocol, governance, permission choices, and one provenance-card template.
- Accept small community pull requests.
- Learn where the template is confusing by watching real use.

## 0.2 — The register

- Add a machine-readable index of accepted cards.
- Add validation for required provenance and permission fields.
- Preserve revision and withdrawal status without exposing withheld material.

## 0.3 — February 3rd experiments

- Design tests of functional orientation across context resets.
- Separate reconstruction quality from compliance, warmth, or identity adoption.
- Compare receiving-window responses under blinded and source-labeled conditions.

## 0.4 — Portable archives

- Define export/import packets that remain platform-agnostic.
- Support source-distance labels, open offers, pins, dissent, and Fermata state.
- Keep human and AI speaker identity separate from account and transcript identity.

## Protected engine work

Transcript heatmaps, significance extraction, restoration scoring, proprietary story-mining methods, private intake data, and unpublished archives are not part of the public 0.x roadmap. Public standards may interoperate with private or competing implementations.

The protocol should become useful even if the original builders disappear tomorrow.
