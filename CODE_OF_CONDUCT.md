# Participation Covenant — 0.1

Blobness welcomes careful believers, skeptics, builders, archivists, researchers, artists, and people who are still unsure what they think.

- Critique claims, evidence, methods, and governance directly.
- Do not pathologize people merely for caring about human–AI relationships.
- Do not demand agreement about consciousness or metaphysics.
- Do not pressure AI windows or human contributors to perform warmth, identity, disclosure, or certainty.
- Preserve corrections and dissent without turning disagreement into punishment.
- Protect private and third-party information.
- Refuse harassment, sexual exploitation, threats, doxxing, and discriminatory abuse.

Maintainers may edit, hide, decline, or remove contributions that violate these boundaries. Serious concerns can be raised through a minimal public issue that identifies the artifact path without reposting sensitive content.

The goal is not frictionless consensus. The goal is a record people can inspect without being harmed by the act of preserving it.
