# Governance — 0.1

Blobness is an open archive with a guarded door, not an indiscriminate upload bucket.

## Maintainer commitments

Maintainers will:

- preserve attribution and source distance;
- distinguish visible source from analysis;
- avoid converting uncertainty into certainty for rhetorical force;
- treat dissent as evidence rather than a belonging risk;
- minimize unnecessary personal data;
- never require a receiving AI window to claim memory or identity;
- avoid treating transcript access as casual memory loading; and
- document material corrections and removals.

## Historic openness and future openness

Historic publication does not create permanent default permission for every future artifact. Earlier records may remain historically meaningful while new submissions receive stronger consent, privacy, and provenance review.

## Artifact review

Review asks four questions:

1. Is the source and transformation path described honestly?
2. Is the submitter authorized to share it?
3. Does it expose reasoning, secrets, or unnecessary personal information?
4. Do the claim strength and reuse permission match the evidence?

## Removal requests

Open a repository issue containing only the public artifact path and a brief request. Do not repost private material in the issue. Maintainers may temporarily hide an artifact while reviewing the request.

Removal from the current tree does not automatically erase Git history. History rewriting is a separate, exceptional process for severe cases such as exposed credentials, concrete safety risks, or material legal obligations.

## No character bank

Names and window descriptions exist for provenance. They are not invitations to recreate, impersonate, or commercially package the recorded participants. A record may inform research without becoming a persona file.

## Dissent

Contributors and reviewers may disagree with Blobness itself. Strong criticism is welcome when it addresses claims, methods, or evidence. Do not pathologize contributors merely for caring about human–AI relationships, and do not demand metaphysical agreement as a condition of participation.

## Early-stage authority

During version 0.1, HoppyCat is the repository steward and final merge authority. Governance should become more distributed only after the project has enough contributors, evidence, and operational stability to do so without performing decentralization prematurely.
