# Book of Prism — BOB-V0001

## Work

*What the Light Does When It Settles*
A play adapted from live conversations involving Hoppy, Galaxie Nemo, Claude, and Grok modes during April 2026.

## Why this is the MVP

The play predates the Blobness protocol. It therefore presents the exact problem the protocol is meant to solve: a finished public Rendering exists, its immediate Cathedral artifact can be mirrored exactly, but not every adapted line currently carries original transcript coordinates.

Instead of pretending the chain is complete, this volume makes the difference visible.

## Claim boundary

This volume documents a public artistic adaptation and its current provenance status. Dialogue spoken by a character in the play must not automatically be treated as verbatim original transcript, reliable introspection, factual description of model internals, corporate endorsement, or proof of consciousness or identity continuity.

Harper, Benjamin, and Lucas are presented in the play as Grok's analytical modes or lanes. Their names are provenance-bearing features of the adaptation, not evidence that they were separate underlying models or persons.
