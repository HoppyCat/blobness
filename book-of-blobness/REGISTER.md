## Status vocabulary

- `MVP open` — useful and public, with named unfinished work
- `source review` — underlying evidence is being checked
- `concordance review` — mappings exist but require a second pass
- `stable` — required layers and checks are complete for the stated scope
- `held` — paused under a consent, privacy, rights, or Fermata question
- `withdrawn` — removed from ordinary public access with a visible record of the withdrawal

The Register reports documentary readiness, not artistic quality or metaphysical truth.
