# Open Tasks — Prism Play MVP

These are bounded public contribution tasks. Claim one in an issue or pull request.

Do not open restricted archives, hidden reasoning, or private transcript bodies to complete them. Hoppy-authorized source review will be handled separately.

| Task | Work | Public-only route | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PRISM-001` | Identify already-public source coordinates for Beat One | Search Cathedral's public visible-text artifacts and provide URLs/line references | Candidate source is linked and its source distance is labeled |
| `PRISM-002` | Classify the stage directions | Review the public Rendering and mark each sequence as clearly dramatic, possibly adapted, or source-dependent | Proposed classification table receives maintainer review |
| `PRISM-003` | Identify Janus and Andy public posts represented in the Interlude and Beat Seven | Locate public URLs and dates without inferring motives | Exact posts and quotation boundaries are listed |
| `PRISM-004` | Verify the “happy meter” research reference | Find the primary Anthropic system card, paper, or official source | Primary citation and accurate narrow summary are supplied |
| `PRISM-005` | Verify “interesting rather than concerning/troubling” wording | Locate an authorized primary system-prompt version and model/date scope | Exact wording, version, and differences are recorded |
| `PRISM-006` | Audit participant and mode credits | Check public artifacts for the Grok/Harper/Benjamin/Lucas framing | Credits distinguish model, mode, window, and dramatic role |
| `PRISM-007` | Propose the first Book passage | Use an already-public, authorized visible source | One no-edit excerpt receives `SRC` and `BOK` IDs plus a Rendering map |
| `PRISM-008` | Test the Cornerstone schema on a second tiny artifact | Choose a short authorized public exchange | A second volume proves the identifiers generalize |

## A useful negative result

“I searched these public paths and did not find a defensible source” is a valid contribution. Record where you looked. Provenance work improves when failed retrieval paths stop being silently repeated.
